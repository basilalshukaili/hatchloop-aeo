## 4. App details (long description — features + benefits)

_(Main body of the listing. Full marketing copy below; a within-limit short version follows in §4a for any field that caps at 500 characters.)_

**Empty and thin product pages cost you sales — and now they cost you AI recommendations too.**

Hatchloop does two jobs in one app: it writes **bulk AI product descriptions** for your whole catalog, and it grades how visible your store is to Google and to AI answer engines like **ChatGPT**, Perplexity, and Gemini — then shows you the exact fixes that move the needle.

**1. AI Product Description Generator**
- Scans your catalog and finds every product with a **blank or thin** description (under ~50 words).
- Generates a clear, **SEO-optimized**, keyword-rich description for each one — typically 80–150 words.
- **Bulk mode**: queue your whole store and generate descriptions in batches instead of editing products one at a time.
- **You preview and approve** before anything touches your store — nothing is published without your click.
- Pushes approved copy live to Shopify via the official Admin API.
- **Flat monthly pricing — no per-word or per-description usage fees.** The AI cost is covered inside your plan.

**2. AI-Visibility & SEO Score**
- Grades your store **0–100** across five dimensions: Discoverability, Product Feed, Schema, Answer-Readiness, and Multi-Market.
- Ranks every recommended fix by **how many points it will gain**, so you always know what to do next.
- Injects **Product, Offer, and FAQPage JSON-LD schema** (structured data) so Google and AI engines can read your catalog (Starter and up).
- Publishes a clean **llms.txt** file so ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity can index your full product range (Starter and up).
- **Citation tracking (Pro)**: see which buyer-intent prompts cite your store across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Copilot, and track your share of voice week over week.

**Why this matters now**
More buyers ask an AI assistant "what's the best ___ for ___?" instead of typing a search query. The stores that get recommended are the ones with complete descriptions and machine-readable schema. If your product pages are thin or unstructured, you're invisible to a fast-growing category of buyer — and to Google's AI Overviews. Hatchloop is built to fix both at once.

**How it works**
1. Install free and run a scan — no setup, no code.
2. See your score and your ranked fix list in minutes.
3. Generate and approve AI descriptions in bulk.
4. Turn on schema + llms.txt and re-scan to watch your score climb.

**Pricing — flat monthly, cancel anytime**
- **Free** — full AI-visibility score, your top 3 ranked fixes, and a preview of 3 AI descriptions.
- **Starter ($12/mo)** — unlimited bulk AI descriptions, all ranked fixes, one-click JSON-LD schema injection, llms.txt enrichment, weekly re-scores.
- **Pro ($79/mo)** — everything in Starter plus citation tracking across ChatGPT / Perplexity / Gemini / Copilot, competitor share-of-voice, and 50 tracked prompts per week.

7-day free trial on paid plans. Manage or cancel from your Shopify admin at any time.
